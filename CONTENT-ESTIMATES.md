## Provisional ranges used on the website

- **Operating window:** likely weekends and school/public holidays, approximately 09:00–17:00; weekday operation is treated as by arrangement. This is a broad scheduling assumption based on the regional comparators, not Splash Kids' published schedule.
- **Child admission:** R70–R120 per child, assuming ages 2–12 solely for budgeting. **Adult admission:** R80–R150 per adult. **Under 2:** R0–R50. These ranges intentionally sit around the small-park examples and below the large destination-park prices; the age bands are estimates, not policy.
- **Family of four:** R300–R540 for two estimated adult tickets plus two estimated child tickets, calculated without assuming a family discount.
- **Groups and parties:** budget the same per-person child/adult estimates; no group discount or package saving is assumed. For birthdays, budget R70–R120 per child for entry, plus any separately estimated lapa fee; catering, cake, décor and exclusive-use options are not included.
- **Lapa booking:** R150–R600 per booking is a wide working allowance only. There is no direct Splash Kids lapa tariff in the source brief; the upper bound is informed by a R600 shelter rental at a larger destination park, not a like-for-like local quote.
- **Parking:** R0–R50 per vehicle, using the City of Johannesburg's R47 municipal-park fee only as an upper-bound reference. Space availability is not guaranteed by this estimate.
- **Offers:** assume 0% discount; do not budget for promotions or group concessions.
- **Food and facilities:** the supplied park information allows visitors to bring food and coolerboxes at no extra charge. For planning, bring food, towels and spare clothes; do not rely on a food outlet, lockers, showers or a dedicated first-aid service unless the park says otherwise.
- **Weather and bookings:** allow for outdoor activity pauses/closures in severe weather; budget conservatively as if tickets are not refundable unless written terms say otherwise. Estimate 3–7 days' lead time for a group or lapa request. These are not official terms or limits on consumer rights.
- **Safety:** no numeric age, height, depth, weight, capacity or supervision limit is estimated. Follow current signs/staff instructions and keep children who cannot swim within arm's reach of a responsible adult.
- **Location:** Google Maps is a search for the supplied street address, not a surveyed entrance pin. Treat the result as approximate.

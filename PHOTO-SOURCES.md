# Activity photo sources

All nine selections are real stock photographs for activity cards. They are representative images, not photographs of Splash Kids Waterpark. Each individual photo page and platform license was checked before selection. The public activity cards do not display photographer or stock-site credit captions; source and license details remain in this project register. The checked Unsplash and Pexels licenses do not require visible attribution.

The activity images are orientation-corrected WebP files at quality 80 with a 1400px maximum edge and no cropping. The nine optimized photos together are about 2.61 MB and are committed under `client/public/images/activities/` so they work on Cloudflare Pages without Manus Storage. The full-resolution source downloads used only for processing are kept out of the runtime build. The owner-supplied brand logo is committed at `client/public/images/brand/splash-kids-logo.webp`.
